### About

Full stack developer specializing in Online Reputation Management (ORM) infrastructure — review collection systems, automation bots, and client-facing SaaS tools. Founder of MonetizeReviews, an ORM agency platform. Design, build, and ship end-to-end: frontend, backend, infra, deployment.

### Current Projects

- **ReviewFlow** — GMB link management platform for vendors and field workers
- **MonetizeReviews** — ORM agency platform for review management at scale
- **NumSphere** — Virtual OTP/SMS marketplace (TypeScript, Framer Motion, Supabase, Redis)
- **Review Scraper Bot** — Telegram bot generating filtered Excel review reports
- **Telegram Food Bot** — Budget-based food recommendation bot
